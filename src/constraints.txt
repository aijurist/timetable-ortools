1.Teacher Course Allocation Constraint
No two courses can be allocated to the same teacher in the same time slot.

2.Daily Teaching Load
A teacher should not be assigned more than 5 hours/day.

3.L-T-P Constraints (Lecture, Tutorial, Practical)
Each course has a fixed number of hours for Lecture (L), Tutorial (T), and Practical (P).
Example: JAVA → L=1, T=2, P=0 ⇒ Total = 3 hours/week.

4.Slot Consecutiveness Constraint
If a teacher is assigned two consecutive slots, a break must be provided afterwards.

5.Slot Timings and Durations
    Slot 1: 8:00–8:50 (no break before)
    Slot 2: 9:00–9:50 (10 min break before)
    Slot 3: Similar setup with 10 min breaks
    upto Slot 11

6.Lab Slot Structuring
    Labs should span two consecutive slots (e.g., 8:00–9:50 with a 10-min break in between).
    upto Slot 6

7.LAB constraints
    FOR 30 students lab (regular lab):
    - If a teacher handles 60 students and practical hours are 2, the teacher must take the lab twice in two batches totaling 4 hours. The two batches must be scheduled on different days.
    - If practical hours are 3 hours, the teacher must take the lab twice in two batches totaling 6 hours, with batches scheduled on different days.
    - A teacher who teaches the theory portion of a course must also teach the practical portion of that same course.
    - If a teacher is assigned the same course twice, they should handle both sections of that course.

    Equipment and Resource Management:
    - Each lab session must have all necessary equipment prepared 30 minutes before class.
    - Lab assistants must be present for complex labs that require additional supervision.

    Special Room Constraints:
    - For labs with 120 students (very large), two teachers must be assigned to the same course. No need for multiple batches.
    - For labs with 60 students (large), one teacher must be assigned. No batches are required.
    - Labs requiring specialized equipment must be scheduled in rooms with appropriate facilities.

8.Special Teacher Assignment
    Some teachers (e.g., lab or specialized) are available only on specific days.
    Allow teachers to specify preferred or unavailable slots (e.g., avoid early morning).

9.Room Availability Constraint
    No two classes should be scheduled in the same room at the same time.

10.Max Consecutive Teaching Hours
    A teacher shouldn't teach for more than 3 hours continuously, even with small breaks.

11.Faculty Idle Time Minimization
    Minimize gaps between classes for a teacher (optimize teacher presence time).

12.Course Spread Constraint
    Distribute L/T/P hours of a course across the week (not all in one day).

13.Student Clash Avoidance
    Ensure students don't have overlapping sessions (especially electives).
